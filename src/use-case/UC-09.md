# Use Case: UC-09
**Use Case ID:** UC-09
**Use Case Name:** Record Material Delivery
**Actor(s):** Supervisor (Mobile App User)

**Preconditions:** 
* Materials have arrived on the construction site.

**Postconditions – Success:** 
A delivery log is saved, updating the site inventory separately from daily usage.

**Main Flow:**
1. The Supervisor navigates to "Material Deliveries".
2. The Supervisor inputs the item name/type that arrived.
3. The Supervisor inputs the delivered quantity.
4. The Supervisor enters the supplier's name and the delivery date.
5. The Supervisor saves the log.
6. The System updates the project's material inventory without affecting the daily "materials used" metric.
