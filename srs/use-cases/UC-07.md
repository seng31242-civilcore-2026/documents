# Use Case: UC-07
**Use Case ID:** UC-07
**Use Case Name:** Log Subcontractor Work
**Actor(s):** Supervisor (Mobile App User)

**Preconditions:**
* Subcontractors and their agreed quotations exist in the system.

**Postconditions – Success:**
Subcontractor work is logged and linked to their quotation.

**Main Flow:**
1. The Supervisor navigates to the "Subcontractors" tab on the daily report.
2. The Supervisor selects a specific subcontractor from the list.
3. The Supervisor links the entry to an agreed quotation.
4. The Supervisor logs the specific work completed by the subcontractor that day.
5. The Supervisor saves the entry.
6. The System updates the subcontractor's completion log for Manager review.
